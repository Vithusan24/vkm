FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, pricing, and booking process.

Are your technicians certified?

Yes, all our technicians undergo strict background checks and hold the necessary certifications and licenses for their respective trades.

Do you provide emergency services?

We offer 24/7 emergency support for critical issues like major plumbing leaks or electrical faults. Extra charges may apply outside normal hours.

How does pricing work?

The prices shown on our website are starting prices. Once you book, our technician will assess the job and provide a final transparent quote before starting any work.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with the service provided, let us know within 24 hours, and we will make it right at no extra cost.
